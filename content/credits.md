# Credits

Tree of Tongues is built on open data. These are the sources we use. Each one keeps its own license. The full register is in data/LICENSES.yaml.

## Data sources

### Glottolog 5.3
Language classification, coordinates and references.

Glottolog 5.3, edited by Harald Hammarström, Robert Forkel, Martin Haspelmath and Sebastian Bank. Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology. https://glottolog.org. DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.18840967](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18840967).

License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). We convert the data into our own formats.

### Unicode CLDR 49
Where languages are spoken, official status and language names in English, Persian and Turkish.

Unicode CLDR 49. Copyright © 2015-2024 Unicode, Inc. Unicode and the Unicode Logo are registered trademarks of Unicode, Inc. in the United States and other countries.

License: [Unicode License v3](https://www.unicode.org/license.txt). The full notice is below.

### Wikidata
Names, speaker estimates and identifiers.

Data from [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org).

License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Images and audio linked from Wikidata have their own licenses. We credit them file by file.

### Natural Earth
Modern country outlines at 1:110m and 1:50m scale, version 5.1.1.

Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com.

License: [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Natural Earth draws borders by who controls the ground. We show disputed areas in a neutral style. Borders on our maps do not mean a language belongs to a state.

## Fonts

All our fonts use the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/). We host them ourselves. Your browser does not fetch them from anyone else. Each font folder holds its license and copyright notice in OFL.txt.

Headings and long reading text.

Tongues Serif is our web version of Source Serif 4, release 4.005R, by Frank Grießhammer for Adobe. We cut it into smaller files for the web and renamed it, as the license asks. Copyright 2014-2023 Adobe, with Reserved Font Name "Source". Source is a trademark of Adobe in the United States and/or other countries.

### Tongues Sans
Interface text.

Tongues Sans is our web version of Source Sans 3, release 3.052R, by Paul D. Hunt for Adobe. We cut it into smaller files for the web and renamed it, as the license asks. Copyright 2010-2022 Adobe, with Reserved Font Name "Source". Source is a trademark of Adobe in the United States and/or other countries.

Adobe did not make or review Tongues Serif or Tongues Sans. Each folder has a FONTLOG.txt that lists our changes.

### Charis
IPA and phonetic text. Version 7.000, used unchanged.

Charis by SIL Global. Copyright 1997-2025 SIL Global, with Reserved Font Names "Charis" and "SIL".

### Noto Naskh Arabic
Arabic script, including Persian.

Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors.

Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors.
