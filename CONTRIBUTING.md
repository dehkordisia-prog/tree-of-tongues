# Contributing to Tree of Tongues

Tree of Tongues is a free, non-commercial, open-source atlas of the world's languages, built for people who love them. Help is welcome.

## Ways to help
- Report an error: open an issue with the page, what is wrong, and a source for the correction.
- Suggest a source: datasets, recordings or maps with a clear open license.
- Record your language: add words on Lingua Libre, or upload recordings to Wikimedia Commons under CC BY or CC BY-SA, then open an issue with the links.
- Translate the interface: Persian and Turkish first, more later.
- Write or review content: follow docs/CONTENT_STYLE.md; every claim needs a source.
- Code: read CLAUDE.md and docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, then open a pull request with tests.

## Ground rules
- No fact without a source; "no data yet" is better than a guess.
- Recordings, reconstructions and synthetic speech are always labeled as such.
- Respect every language community: no stereotypes, no sacred or restricted material.
- Contributions are released under the project's licenses: MIT for code, CC BY-SA 4.0 for writing.
